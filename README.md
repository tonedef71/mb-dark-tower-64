Coded in Commodore BASIC V2 for the C64, this game is a single-player adaptation of the original 1981 [Milton Bradley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milton_Bradley_Company) [Dark Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_Tower_(game)) electronic board game hybrid.  It is derived from the original "*Obsidian Spire*" game listing from [Edwin Nagle](mailto:james.nagle@computesgazette.com) that appeared in the [February 26th edition](https://shop.computesgazette.com/product/computes-gazette-issue-8-digital-edition/) of his [Compute's Gazette Magazine](https://www.computesgazette.com/).  It preserves many game play ideas introduced in *Obsidian Spire*, such as the all-text visuals and grid-like kingdoms with the north/south/east/west movement, but it also re-implements other game play aspects to more closely resemble the *Dark Tower* board game it is based on.  It further expands on *Obsidian Spire* by adapting more game features from *Dark Tower*, such as the _Wizard of Curses_ and the _Flying Pegasus_, and it adds additional sound effects and music that resemble those from the original *Dark Tower* board game.

### TIP:
The game's random events can be mercilessly brutal.  Upon starting a new game, immediately make a beeline to the bazaar and purchase a healer there; the ROI is substantial.  Next, purchase a scout as well.  You can purchase a beast later to bear more gold for hiring expendable mercenaries and buying food to prevent those mercenaries from dying of painful starvation instead of from glorious combat.
