Coded in Commodore BASIC V2 for the C64, this game is a single-player adaptation of the original 1981 [Milton Bradley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milton_Bradley_Company) [Dark Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_Tower_(game)) electronic board game hybrid.  It is derived from the original "*Obsidian Spire*" game listing from [Edwin Nagle](mailto:james.nagle@computesgazette.com) that appeared in the [February 26th edition](https://shop.computesgazette.com/product/computes-gazette-issue-8-digital-edition/) of his [Compute's Gazette Magazine](https://www.computesgazette.com/).  It preserves many game play ideas introduced in *Obsidian Spire*, such as the all-text visuals and grid-like kingdoms with the north/south/east/west movement, but it also re-implements other game play aspects to more closely resemble the *Dark Tower* board game it is based on.  It further expands on *Obsidian Spire* by adapting more game features from *Dark Tower*, such as the _Wizard of Curses_ and the _Flying Pegasus_, and it adds additional sound effects and music that resemble those from the original *Dark Tower* board game.

### Premise
The magic scepter, a legendary relic of immense power, has fallen into the clutches of the evil tyrant king, who has absconded away with it to the dread Dark Tower, a daunting fortress protected by a gate unlockable only with three magic keys.  From his throne room within the Dark Tower, the tyrant king uses the scepter to summon hordes of brutish brigands, dark and sinister wizards, ferocious dragons, miserable plagues, and thick mists of bewildering disorientation to plunge the four free kingdoms of the realm into hopelessness and despair.  As the brave champion of the Kingdom of Arisilon, you vow to restore peace and prosperity back to the realm by recovering the magic scepter and ending the evil tyrant king's reign.  To achieve victory, you must overcome the deadly obstacles imperiling the realm, build a formidable army of warriors and allies, find the magic key hidden within each of the three neighboring kingdoms, and then march your army onward to storm the Dark Tower and defeat the evil guarding it.

### TIPS:
The game's random events can be mercilessly brutal.  Upon starting a new game, immediately make a beeline to the bazaar and purchase a healer there; the ROI is substantial.  Next, purchase a scout as well.  You may later purchase a beast of burden to bear more gold (for hiring expendable mercenaries and buying food to prevent those mercenaries from dying of painful starvation instead of dying in glorious battle).

The dragon only attacks in open terrain spaces but avoids a kingdom's border territories.

Before storming the tower, return to the citadel in your home kingdom of Arisilon for reinforcements.  If there are between 5 and 24 warriors in your army, the number of warriors will then be doubled.

The mystical pegasus can fly you and your army to any primary location within your home kingdom or any kingdom you have obtained the key for.

The wizard will dispel curses invoked by any dark wizards in the realm and/or may be sacrificed to summon a random number of gold and warriors.
